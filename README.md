# Pokémon Legends: Arceus Challenge

Checklist for a challenge run of Pokémon Legends: Arceus.

### The Challenge: My Custom Rules

The primary goal is to complete every item on this checklist, without trading and without Pokémon Home.

This section lists some custom rules I'm setting for myself in this challenge. Most of them make the challenge easier, as it's pretty damn difficult to make the challenge any harder than it already is.

I've also changed the checklist to remove some of the less fun, more grindy aspects;

- I don't have to level every Pokémon to level 100 or max out their effort levels.
- I don't have to catch the shiny and shalpha variant of every Pokémon (though I'll probably give it a go once I finish the challenge, and I'll still track any shinies I do happen to come across).

This is an adaptation of a (much harder) challenge sheet I created, you can find the original [here](https://gist.github.com/WillsterJohnson/37fb7f57f228c678d0ad4dbf02244670).

To do this challenge yourself, download this file, and do a find-and-replace to swap `[x]` for `[ ]`, then open the file in [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) or your favorite markdown editor.

#### Rule One: Devout Arceusist

Unlike the natives of this land, who worship their false god 'Sinnoh', I worship none other than our Lord and Creator Arceus.

To appease our great Lord, I must go above and beyond in my divine quest to Seek Out All Pokémon by completing as much of the challenge as is possible to do before facing Arceus.

#### Rule Two: Distracted Hero

I am easily sidetracked and constantly losing focus of my current goals.

I must find and complete all side-quests / requests before advancing the story, unless advancing the story is the only way to complete the side-quest.

#### Rule Three: No Signal

I just fell out of a wormhole and landed firmly in the past. I don't know how long in the past, but it's definitely before the internet was invented.

I am not allowed to use any online features in the game for any reason. This means that if I blackout, those items are *gone*.

#### Rule Four: Checkmate, Einstein

Returning to a point in spacetime to try things from a different angle? No mere mortal can do that!

Unless I have both Palkia and Dialga in my party, I am not allowed to save-reset. Not even if I KO a shiny.

#### Rule Five: Ingo's Infinite Inventory

> The purpose of this allowance is so I can spend more time actually playing the game rather than spending hours upon hours grinding for Pokébucks or the materials for Pokéballs and sticky globs.
> I still have to grind for Pokébucks/materials if I want to buy anything else.

I'm allowed to use the Ingo pasture glitch to obtain excessive quantities of grit items. I may not use any grit items obtained via Ingo, and must immediately sell them. The Pokébucks received are considered to be 'glitched'.

Glitched Pokébucks can only be used to buy Pokéballs (any variant) and sticky globs.
Once my wallet contains any glitched Pokébucks (even just one!), my entire wallet is considered glitched; to un-glitch my wallet, I must dump as much of my money as possible into Pokéballs and sticky globs, until I'm below ₽100 (i.e., the cost of a regular Pokéball).

If I am ever in doubt of whether my wallet is glitched, I must assume that it is glitched and dump my money as described above.

Money laundering is not allowed; for example, I cannot sell the Pokéballs I get from dumping a glitched wallet. 

Any loopholes in the above rules are not allowed to be exploited.
